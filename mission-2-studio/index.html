Your design, in five steps.

  1. Explore. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Try the camera presets.
  2. Select. Click a model part or its name in Scene objects. Use Select whole model to resize everything.
  3. Customize. Change colors and materials. Use Move, Rotate or Resize handles, or enter values in the inspector. New image references stay separate from geometry.
  4. Save. Save project keeps this browser’s copy. Download editable backup for a portable copy with embedded textures and references.
  5. Confirm. Validate and download Venture_3D_Confirmed_Handoff.zip. Carry the entire ZIP into Mission 3.

Starting models are stylized geometry created during development. This studio doesn’t reconstruct arbitrary images into accurate 3D models.

Coach parts are rigid joints. Playing its animation can override edits to animated joints; edit the body meshes or leave playback paused for a static pose.
